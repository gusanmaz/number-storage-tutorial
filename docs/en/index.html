This sentence, a photo, a song, every app on your phone: all of it is billions of tiny switches, each on or off. This site shows, step by step, how meaning is made out of those switches.

Tap a lamp. A letter changes. That is the whole story.

What is this site about?

Computers know only two things: on and off. Yet they write text, show colors, play music and fly spacecraft. We explain how that is possible not with tables to memorize but with pieces you can pick up and tinker with. In every chapter you will tap lamps, drag the orange numbers inside the text, and practice with exercises that generate their own questions.

The chapters are written to be read in order; each builds on the previous one. But you can also start wherever you are curious. For high school and university students, programming beginners, and anyone who has ever wondered “what is really going on inside a computer?”.
